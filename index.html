Odd Lore Club

Weird true stories from history, science & legend. Every story has a twist.

Odd Lore Club is a channel of short animated videos about weird true stories from history, science and legend, told by three cartoon hosts: Tick, a pocket watch who hosts history; Professor Hoot, an owl who hosts science; and Nova, an explorer who hosts legends.

Every story is checked against multiple sources, and the sources are listed in each video's description. The animation is original; the narration voices are AI-generated.
