Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 (added TikTok, Instagram, Facebook)

This policy covers Odd Lore Club Publisher ("the app"), a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Odd Lore Club to manage that channel and its own accounts on TikTok (@oddloreclub), Instagram (@oddloreclub) and the Odd Lore Club Facebook Page. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, and only with the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app may:

  • upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags, subtitles, playlists and publishing schedule on the owner's channel;
  • read the owner's channel, playlist and video information.

TikTok

With the owner's permission (Login with TikTok), the app may upload and publish the channel's own videos to the @oddloreclub TikTok account, with the caption, privacy and interaction settings the owner chooses, and read basic profile information (display name, avatar) to show which account is connected. The app does not access any other TikTok user's data.

Instagram and Facebook

Through Meta's official APIs the app publishes the channel's own Reels and captions to the @oddloreclub Instagram account and the Odd Lore Club Facebook Page, and reads their basic statistics.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the channel's own videos and check that they were posted correctly.
  • Login tokens are stored privately on the owner's own computer and are never published or shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect any data about viewers.

Third-party terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps & services, or through Google security settings. When access is revoked, the stored login token stops working and is deleted within 7 days; any information obtained from YouTube API Services is deleted or refreshed at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies. Any stored data is also deleted on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel through Odd Lore Club on YouTube.
